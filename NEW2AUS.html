Primary Identity Documents

  • Passport (current, bio-data page)
  • Birth certificate (original or certified copy)
  • Driver licence (if available)

Secondary Identity Documents

  • Utility bill or bank statement (within 3 months)
  • Medicare card or private health insurance details
  • Proof of address (rental agreement or official letter)
Important: Anyone working with children (paid or volunteer) must apply for a Working With Vulnerable People/Working With Children card — for example, the WWVP Card via Access Canberra or your local state/territory government service centre. Check your state/territory requirements and apply before commencing child-related work.

Vehicle Details: Buying, Registration & Ownership

Buying new vs used: new cars give warranty & certainty while used cars may offer savings—always obtain a roadworthy check for used vehicles. Registration is state-based; ensure you transfer title and check any outstanding finance. Owner sales & dealerships both have pros—get receipts and check historical reports.

Transport — Interstate Travel & Public Transit

Interstate travel: flights (Qantas, Virgin) are fastest between capitals; long-distance trains/coaches are available for budget travel. Local public transit fares vary by city (Opal, Myki, Translink, MyWay). Use local travel cards/apps for best fares; consider concession rules if eligible.
