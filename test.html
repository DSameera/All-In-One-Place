Identity Documents — Primary & Secondary

Primary Identity Documents

  • Passport (current, bio-data page)
  • Birth certificate (original or certified copy)
  • Driver licence (if available)

Secondary Identity Documents

  • Utility bill or bank statement (within 3 months)
  • Medicare card or private health insurance details
  • Proof of address (rental agreement or official letter)
Important: Anyone working with children (paid or volunteer) must apply for a Working With Vulnerable People / Working With Children card — e.g., the WWVP Card via Access Canberra or your local state/territory service centre.
